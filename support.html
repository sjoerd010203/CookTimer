CookTimer Support

Help with the CookTimer app for iPhone and iPad.

Contact us

For questions, feedback, or problems with the app, email us at wildsproutstudios.support@gmail.com.

Reporting a problem

Please tell us what you were doing when the problem happened and, if you can, how to make it happen again. It also helps to include your app version (open Settings → About inside CookTimer and look at Version), your device model, and your iOS version. A screenshot is welcome too.

Frequently asked

  • Do I need an account? No. CookTimer has no accounts. Your timers, favorites and notes stay on your device.
  • I don't get an alert when a step finishes. Make sure notifications are on for CookTimer in your device's Settings app under Notifications. Focus modes, such as Do Not Disturb, can silence them. In CookTimer, open Settings (the gear button) and check that Sound and Vibrate are on.
  • How do I stop waiting for each step? Timers always wait for your Start. To move through the steps automatically, open Settings in CookTimer and turn off "Start each step myself".
  • What are the "check" prompts? Some steps, like chicken or steak, ask you to check the food before moving on. You choose °C or °F in Settings.
  • How do I remove ads? Open Settings in CookTimer and tap "Remove ads". It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If you already bought it, tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • How do I share a timer, or import one? In Settings, choose "Share timers", pick the ones you want and send the message. To import, copy the whole message you received, then open Settings and tap the Paste button under "Share and import".
  • How do I use the widget? Add the CookTimer widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows your favorites, which you can save with the heart button. Tap one to start it. Allow notifications so you get alerts, and open CookTimer once so it can show the timer on your Lock Screen.
  • The Lock Screen timer doesn't appear. In your device's Settings app, open CookTimer and check that Live Activities is on.
  • How do I delete my data? Everything is stored on your device, so deleting the app removes your timers, favorites and notes.
  • How do I change my ad choices? In regions where it is required, Settings in CookTimer shows "Ad privacy choices".
