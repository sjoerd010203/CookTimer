Privacy Policy

This policy describes what information CookTimer collects, how it's used, and your choices. In short: CookTimer has no accounts and no servers of our own, and we do not collect your personal information. The app does show ads, which are provided by Google (see "Advertising" below).

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Your content: your favorite timers, timers you create, notes you write, recently used timers, and timers that are currently running.
  • Your settings: for example appearance, sound, vibration, temperature unit, and whether each step waits for you to start it.
  • This information stays on your device. It is also available to CookTimer's own widget through storage on the same device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us.

Notifications and Lock Screen Timers

  • Alerts when a step finishes are local notifications created by the app on your device. They do not use a server of ours.
  • You can turn notifications and Live Activities off at any time in your device's Settings app.

Clipboard

  • CookTimer reads your clipboard only when you tap the Paste button in Settings to import a shared timer.

Advertising

  • CookTimer shows a full-screen ad after a timer finishes, using Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device type, IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google's own privacy practices apply to that information.
  • CookTimer does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app, where that option is available.
  • You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage Google's ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops ads from being shown.

Purchases

  • The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Apple through your Apple ID. We never receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether the purchase is active and remembers the result on your device.

Sharing Timers

  • If you share a timer, the app creates a text message with the timer's name, steps, and any note you wrote, and you choose where to send it. Once someone receives it, we have no control over it.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not have your personal information to sell.
  • Information leaves your device only in the ways described above: ads provided by Google, purchases handled by Apple, and anything you choose to share yourself.

Your Choices

  • Because everything you create is stored on your device, deleting the app removes your timers, favorites, notes, and settings.
  • You can change your ad choices as described above, or remove ads with a one-time purchase.
  • If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have rights over your personal data, such as access and deletion. Since we hold none, requests about advertising data are best sent to Google. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
